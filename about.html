Who We Are

Our Journey Since 2017

Founded in 2017, M/s DEV Enterprises has grown into a specialized industrial service contractor delivering painting, protective coating and flooring solutions for demanding industrial environments.

Our work is guided by Quality, Safety, Delivery, Cost, Morale and Environment. We focus on practical execution, reliable workmanship and solutions suited to pharmaceutical, chemical, packaging, engineering and other industrial facilities.

Mission

To deliver dependable industrial painting, coating and flooring solutions with disciplined execution, quality workmanship and strong safety practices.

Vision

To build long-term client relationships by becoming a trusted industrial service partner known for reliability, consistency and professional execution.

Core Values

Quality, Safety, Delivery, Cost, Morale and Environment remain central to how we plan, execute and review our work.
